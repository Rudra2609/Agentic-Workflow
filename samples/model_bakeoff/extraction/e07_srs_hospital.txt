IT-314 Software Engineering — Lab 5
Requirements and Design for a Hospital Appointment System

This lab does not involve coding. Your team will produce requirement and design documents for an online hospital appointment system.

The system to be described must let patients register, book, reschedule and cancel appointments with doctors; let doctors view their daily schedule and mark appointments as completed; and let administrators manage doctors' working hours. Patients must receive an SMS or email reminder 24 hours before an appointment.

Documents to produce:
1. A Software Requirements Specification following the IEEE 830 structure, including at least 15 functional and 5 non-functional requirements.
2. A use-case diagram covering all three actors, with at least 3 fully written use-case descriptions.
3. A class diagram and one sequence diagram for the booking flow.
4. A traceability matrix linking requirements to use cases.

Use any UML tool (draw.io, StarUML, PlantUML). Diagrams must be exported as PNG and embedded in a single PDF. Teams have 4 or 5 members; every member's contribution must be listed on the cover page.

Evaluation: SRS 35 marks, use cases 25 marks, class and sequence diagrams 25 marks, traceability matrix 15 marks.
