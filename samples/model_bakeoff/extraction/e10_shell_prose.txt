hi everyone, quick note about the OS mini project since a few of you asked after class. you'll be writing your own
unix shell in C. it should run external programs with their arguments, obviously, and it has to handle a pipe between
two commands like ls | wc -l. please also do input and output redirection with < and >, and running a job in the
background with & at the end of the line. you need the built-ins cd, exit and history (history just prints the last 10
commands). pressing ctrl+c should stop the running program but must never kill the shell itself.

you must use fork, exec and wait yourselves - calling system() anywhere means zero for that part. submit the source
with a Makefile, and you'll demo it to the TA during the lab viva.

marking: half the marks (50) are for running commands and pipes, 20 for redirection, 15 for the built-ins and history,
and the remaining 15 for signal handling. good luck!
