Distributed Systems — Term Paper
Topic: Consensus Algorithms in Practice

Write a survey-style report comparing Paxos, Raft and one Byzantine fault tolerant protocol of your choice (for example PBFT or HotStuff).

Your report must:
1. Explain how each algorithm reaches agreement, including leader election where relevant.
2. State the fault model of each algorithm (crash faults vs Byzantine faults) and how many failures it tolerates.
3. Compare the message complexity of the three algorithms in normal operation.
4. Describe at least two real systems that use each of Paxos and Raft.
5. Discuss one known limitation or failure case for each algorithm.

The report should be between 3000 and 4000 words. Cite at least 8 sources, of which at least 4 must be peer-reviewed papers. Use IEEE citation style.

Submit the report as a PDF and a one-slide summary (PPTX or PDF) for the in-class discussion.

Evaluation
Technical accuracy — 40%
Depth of comparison — 25%
Use of sources and citations — 15%
Writing quality and structure — 20%
